// About me

Systems thinker, builder at heart

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Linköping University, specialising in software development with a side of embedded systems. I like understanding how things work from the ground up — whether that's a network protocol, a video codec, or an OS scheduler.

Alongside my studies I work as a System Engineer at Trafikverket's R&D lab, where I design and build proof-of-concepts in areas ranging from real-time video streaming to AI-assisted document search.

I'm especially drawn to IT security, systems programming, and developer tooling. I enjoy challenges that require both analytical depth and clean implementation.
